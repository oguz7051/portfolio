Mechanical subsystem · TÜRKSAT Model Satellite Competition 2024 · Team NEBULA-S

Model satellite and carrier

I did the whole mechanical subsystem on my own: primary structure, layout of the PCBs, battery and camera, the carrier and its separation mechanism, and the landing protection for the colour filters on the bottom face. Parts were 3D-printed in PLA and assembled by hand. The Preliminary Design Review scored 91.87/100.

Design 1 · PDR

Legs that take the landing

The filters and camera sat on the bottom face, so touchdown could not hit them. Fixed legs with stiff spring-loaded feet absorbed the impact. We tested the landing with the hardware.

Design 2 · CDR

Lighter: antennas as landing contacts

For the critical design I removed the legs and used the antenna tips as landing contacts, which made the satellite lighter. Next time I would film and log every test so the two designs can be compared with numbers.

Mechanical team leader · TEKNOFEST Rocket Competition

Mid-altitude rocket (1,200 m)

I designed the payload module, bulkheads and the four fins, and prepared the fins for laser cutting and CNC. The body tubes and nose cone were made in fiberglass and epoxy by vacuum-assisted hand layup. I predicted apogee and stability with OpenRocket.
